How to Play Quarto

Each piece has four attributes:

  • Tall or Short
  • Light or Dark
  • Round or Square
  • Hollow or Solid

On your turn, you choose a piece for your opponent.

Your opponent must place that piece somewhere on the board.

Then they choose a piece for you.

Create a row, column or diagonal of four pieces sharing ANY attribute to win.
